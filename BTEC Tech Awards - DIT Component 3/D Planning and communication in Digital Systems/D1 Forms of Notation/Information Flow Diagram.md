- Shows how information is communicated between people, departments, or systems
- Compared to a [[Data Flow Diagrams|Data flow Diagram]]:
	- It is broader than a [[Data Flow Diagrams|DFD]], as it focuses on
		- Communication paths
		- Who receives information
		- Security concerns
- It is used in *organisational communication mapping*, *cybersecurity*, *risk analysis,* *network or enterprise infrastructure*.
