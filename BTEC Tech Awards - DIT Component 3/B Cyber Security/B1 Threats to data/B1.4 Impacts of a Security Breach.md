### Impacts of a Security Breach
- ***Data loss***
	- Important files, customer information or business records may be stolen, deleted, or corrupted. 
- ***Damage to public image***
	- Customers and partners may lose trust if they believe the organisation cannot protect sensitive information
- ***Reduction in productivity***
	- Staff may be unable to work normally while systems are being investigated or restored. Time is spent fixing the issue instead of completing usual tasks.  
- ***Financial loss***
	- A breach often results in direct costs (investigating the attack, repairing systems, paying fines) and indirect costs (lost customers, reduced sales).  
- ***Downtime***
	- Systems or networks may be unavailable for hours or days, stopping business operations until the breach is resolved.  
- ***Legal action***
	- Organisations may face lawsuits, regulatory penalties, or compensation claims if they failed to protect personal data or meet legal requirements such as GDPR
