### Internal Threats to digital systems and data security
- An **internal threat** in the context of cybersecurity is a risk that comes from within an organisation, rather than from external attackers like hackers or criminal groups.
- An internal threat is caused by people who *already have authorised access to a system,* such as:
	- Employees, students, contractors, volunteers, temporary staff
	  
- #### They are dangerous because:
	- Insiders already have access permissions
	- Their actions may not trigger security alerts
	- They understand how systems work
	- Damage can happen quickly and quietly
	  
- #### Examples of internal threats
	- Intentional stealing or leaking of information 
	- Users overriding security controls 
	- Unintentional disclosure of data to a third-party
	- *Use of portable storage devices* 
	- *Downloads from internet* 
	- *Visiting untrustworthy websites*
