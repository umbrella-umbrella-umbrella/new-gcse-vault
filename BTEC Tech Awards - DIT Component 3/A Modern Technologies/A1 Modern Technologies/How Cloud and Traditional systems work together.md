### How Cloud and "Traditional" systems work together
- A *"Traditional"* system is where Software and Data are stored locally on your own computer, or the organisation's servers.
  
- #### Examples of them working together:
	- Office Online acts as a free almost "trial" version of the full-featured Office desktop suite.
	- Git is installed on your own computer, and communicates to the GitHub website which stores your files remotely as a form of Version Control.
	- It "pushes" your files to GitHub, and can "pull" files from it.
