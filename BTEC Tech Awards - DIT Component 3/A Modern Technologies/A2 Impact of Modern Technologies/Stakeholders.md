- A stakeholder is any individual, group, or organisation that has an interest in, or is affected by, the *activities, decisions, or outcomes* of a *project, business, or organisation*.
	- Examples of stakeholders, and their objectives:
		- **Investors / Shareholders**
			- Want to see potential return on investment.
		- **Advisors / Mentors**
			- Help by offering advice and measure progress.
		- **Lenders / Banks**
			- Concerned with risk and repayment ability.
		- **Government**
			- Interest in legal compliance such as tax.
		- **Employees**
			- Interested on whether invoices/bills are paid on time. 
		- **Customers**
			- Expect a consistent service to meet their needs.
		- **Suppliers**
			- Interested on whether invoices/bills are paid on time. 
		- **Owners / Founders**
			- Interested in businesses stability/growth.
	- #### *The 2 types of Investors:*
		- Internal
			- (inside the organisation) – e.g., employees, managers, owners.
		- External
			- (outside the organisation) – e.g., customers, suppliers, investors, government, community.
