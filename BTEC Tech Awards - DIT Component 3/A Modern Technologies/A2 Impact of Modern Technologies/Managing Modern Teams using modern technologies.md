### How modern technologies can be used to manage [[Changes to Modern Teams|Modern Teams]]
- Collaboration software is used in [[Changes to Modern Teams]] and is designed to help people working in a team to achieve their objectives.
	- **Collaboration Tools** allows team members to work together on shared tasks, documents, and projects, even when in different locations.
	- **Communication Tools** help team members stay connected, share updates, and discuss ideas efficiently
	- **Scheduling Tools** are used to organise meetings, manage time zones, and set reminders so everyone stays on track.
	- **Planning Tools:** help teams plan projects, assign tasks, and monitor progress over time.
