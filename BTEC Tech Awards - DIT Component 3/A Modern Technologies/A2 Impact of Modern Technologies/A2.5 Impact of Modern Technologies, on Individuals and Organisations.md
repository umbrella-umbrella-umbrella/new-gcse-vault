### Impact of Modern Technologies
- The use of modern technology can have an impact on individuals and organisations.
- Examples of modern technology include:
	- [[A1.1 Communication Technologies (Networks)|Communication Technologies]]
	- Devices (e.g Smartphones, Tablets, PCs)
	- [[A1.5 How Cloud and Traditional systems work together|Local]] and [[Cloud Technologies, Systems|Web-based platforms]] (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365)

--- 
#### Impacts on organisations

| Area                                                                                                                        | Positive                                                                                                 | Negative                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Required infrastructure** ([[A1.1 Communication Technologies (Networks)\|Comms Tech]], devices, local and web-based platforms) | Enables communication, remote access and shared platforms (e.g. Teams, Zoom, cloud storage)              | Costly to buy, set up, maintain and train staff on                                                                                              |
| **Demand on infrastructure** of chosen tools                                                                                | Scalable cloud tools can cope with growth                                                                | Many users or large files can slow networks and need more bandwidth and servers; the tools chosen drive what must be bought                     |
| **Availability of infrastructure**                                                                                          | Cloud services are accessible from anywhere with a connection                                            | Outages, poor broadband or no signal stop work; reliance on third-party providers                                                               |
| **24/7 access or security of distributed data**                                                                             | Staff can reach data any time, in any location, with backups held off-site                               | Data held across many devices and locations is easier to lose or steal. Risks include hacking, weak passwords, unsecured Wi-Fi and lost devices |
| **Collaboration or inclusivity** (age, health, additional needs, multicultural)                                             | Shared documents and video calls let diverse and distant teams work together; recruits from a wider pool | Older or less confident staff may struggle; language and time-zone barriers; staff may feel isolated                                            |
| **Accessibility** (legal obligations, provision requirements)                                                               | Tools such as screen readers, captions and speech-to-text help meet the Equality Act 2010                | Organisations must provide and pay for adjustments; non-compliance risks legal action                                                           |
| **Remote working**                                                                                                          | Lower office costs, wider talent pool, better staff retention                                            | Harder to monitor and manage staff, weaker team culture, security risks, and the employer must support home set-ups                             |

--- 
#### Impacts on individuals

| Area                                                  | Positive                                                                                                                        | Negative                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Flexibility** (home/remote working)                 | Better work–life balance, saves time and money commuting                                                                        | Work can spill into home life; hard to switch off                                                                        |
| **Working styles** (choice of time, device, location) | Staff choose when, where and on what device they work, which can boost productivity                                             | Needs self-discipline; distractions at home; may need to use personal devices (BYOD) and pay for their own connection    |
| **Mental wellbeing**                                  | Feel in control of their schedule; schedule can fit family needs; less stress from commuting; escape from a stressful workplace | Loneliness, isolation, depression, lower self-confidence, less face-to-face support, and pressure to always be available |
